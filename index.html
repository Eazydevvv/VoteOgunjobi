Research Excellence & Global Visibility

  • Encourage interdisciplinary research
  • Expand international collaborations
02

Postgraduate Transformation

  • Improve postgraduate mentoring
  • Facilitate timely completion of programmes
  • Enhance research infrastructure
  • Promote postgraduate funding opportunities
03

Staff Development & Welfare

  • Facilitate staff capacity building
  • Encourage mentorship for early-career academics

Student Success Initiative

Modern Scientific Infrastructure

  • Improve maintenance culture

Industry & Community Engagement

  • Facilitate internships and industrial attachments
  • Promote community-based scientific solutions

Digital Faculty Initiative

  • Enhance e-learning and digital administration
  • Support data-driven decision-making
  • Improve online visibility of faculty achievements

Resource Mobilization

  • Seek external grants and endowments
  • Engage alumni and development partners
  • Encourage research commercialization

Harmonious Faculty Community

Scientific Leadership for National Development
